## Topic 1.2



### EN before



Baybayin is not an alphabet like the one we use today. It is a SYLLABARY. Each character represents a syllable, unlike an alphabet, where each letter represents a sound.

It comes from the root word “baybáy,” meaning to spell or divide words into syllables.



### EN after



Baybayin is not an alphabet like the one we use today. It is a SYLLABARY. Each character represents a syllable, unlike an alphabet, where each letter represents a sound.

It comes from the root word “baybáy,” meaning to spell or divide words into syllables.



Takeaway: One character, one syllable! That's how Baybayin works.



### TL before



Ang Baybayin ay hindi alpabeto na tulad ng ginagamit natin ngayon kundi SILABARYO. Bawat simbolo nito ay katumbas ng isang pantig (syllable), hindi tulad ng ating alpabeto na bawat titik ay isang tunog.

Mula sa ugat na salitang “baybáy” o ang pagbabaybay ng mga salita (to spell, to syllabicate).



### TL after

## Topic 1.4



### EN before



Did you notice?

Sa Baybayin, iisa lang ang simbolo para sa E at I (ᜁ), at iisa rin para sa O at U (ᜂ).

Bakit kaya? Noong unang panahon, malayang nagpapalitan ang tunog ng E at I, pati na rin ng O at U, nang hindi nagbabago ang kahulugan ng salita!



### EN after



Did you notice?

In Baybayin, E and I share one character (ᜁ), and O and U share one character (ᜂ).

Why? In the past, the sounds E and I, and O and U, were interchangeable without changing a word's meaning!



Takeaway: They share one character because their sounds are similar!

## Topic 1.5



### EN before



Another surprise!

Ang tunog ng Da at Ra ay iisa lang din ang simbolo sa Baybayin (ᜇ).

Sa ating wika, ang titik D ay madalas maging R kapag napagigitnaan ng mga patinig. Kaya sa sinaunang pagsulat, iisang simbolo lang ang kailangan para sa dalawang tunog na ito!



### EN after



Another surprise!

Da and Ra also share one Baybayin character (ᜇ).

In our language, D often becomes R when it is between vowels. So in ancient writing, only one character was needed for these two sounds!



Takeaway: Are you ready to meet the characters?
