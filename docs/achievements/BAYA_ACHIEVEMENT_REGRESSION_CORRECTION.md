## Scope and provenance

The compact writing screen, shared Settings overlay, the larger swipe gesture and published combined audio mapping predate these corrections. The four native SQLite errors arose from the achievement-aware playback owner lookup. One unhandled asynchronous error can surface under an adjacent example-word test. The isolated viewer suite now supplies an owner dependency and still exercises playback cancellation. The awaiting-validation fixture still exercises a genuinely unmapped prompt.

The production corrections affect CharacterViewer.tsx, usePronunciation.ts, characterActivityService.ts, assessment.ts and the portrait route allowance in app/_layout.tsx. The nine suites in the table contain the expectation or fixture changes. The populated-database upgrade and idempotency case lives in database/__tests__/achievements.test.ts. The new historical DE deferral case rejects new submissions while preserving the earlier unscored result.

The first archived comparison failed to initialize because its public model directory was missing. Those initialization failures do not establish pre-achievement behavior. The original checkout remained intact during comparison.

A second isolated run against the archived pre-achievement source used the public and assets directories from the workspace. It ran 372 tests and reproduced 15 of the original 19 by suite and full test name. R06 through R09 passed on that baseline. The baseline also failed a separate 360 px workspace-actions assertion that passed in the corrected suite. The adjacent lesson SwipeHint suite also matched the filename pattern. See [baseline comparison](../verification/achievements/regression/baseline.json).
