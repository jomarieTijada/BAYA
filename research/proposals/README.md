# Proposals

## Purpose

Documents thesis proposals and approved study plans for the BAYA thesis.

## Responsibilities

Plans research questions, methods, scope, revisions, approvals, and alignment to BAYA objectives.

## Allowed Contents

Future ethics-approved, access-controlled materials specifically supporting thesis proposals and approved study plans, plus documentation now.

## Excluded Contents

Operational application records, unrelated ML datasets, credentials, unnecessary identities, and actual respondent records during this task.

## Dependencies

Uses only approved research inputs and privacy-reviewed exports with traceable study and dataset versions.

## Related BAYA Requirements

Supports thesis proposals and approved study plans while preserving separation among respondent, ISO 25010, algorithm, ML training, and operational data.

## Future Implementation Notes

Anonymize or pseudonymize personal and research data where appropriate and follow consent, retention, and access rules.
