# Ethics

## Purpose

Documents research ethics governance for the BAYA thesis.

## Responsibilities

Plans consent, data minimization, participant protection, access, withdrawal, retention, and approval records.

## Allowed Contents

Future ethics-approved, access-controlled materials specifically supporting research ethics governance, plus documentation now.

## Excluded Contents

Operational application records, unrelated ML datasets, credentials, unnecessary identities, and actual respondent records during this task.

## Dependencies

Uses only approved research inputs and privacy-reviewed exports with traceable study and dataset versions.

## Related BAYA Requirements

Supports research ethics governance while preserving separation among respondent, ISO 25010, algorithm, ML training, and operational data.

## Future Implementation Notes

Anonymize or pseudonymize personal and research data where appropriate and follow consent, retention, and access rules.
