# Research

## Purpose

Separates thesis research governance and evidence from BAYA operational application data and ML production logic.

## Responsibilities

Maintains distinct areas for proposals, instruments, respondent data, algorithm results, system and ISO 25010 evaluation, usability, statistics, ethics, and exports.

## Allowed Contents

Documentation now; future approved, access-controlled, minimized, and versioned research materials.

## Excluded Contents

Operational learner records, ML training data stored without dataset governance, application databases, credentials, and actual respondent records during this task.

## Dependencies

Research may consume privacy-reviewed exports from operational or ML areas but must preserve purpose, consent, access, and lineage boundaries.

## Related BAYA Requirements

Supports defensible thesis evaluation while separating operational application data, ML training data, respondent data, ISO 25010 data, and algorithm-comparison results.

## Future Implementation Notes

Personal and research data must be anonymized or pseudonymized where appropriate. No respondent records are created now.
