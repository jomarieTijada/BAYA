## Purpose

Documents learner usability study materials for the BAYA thesis.

## Responsibilities

Plans future task outcomes, observations, ratings, accessibility findings, and pseudonymized qualitative feedback.

Supports learner usability study materials while preserving separation among respondent, ISO 25010, algorithm, ML training, and operational data.

## Future Implementation Notes
