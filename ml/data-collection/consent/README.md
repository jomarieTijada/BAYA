# Consent

## Purpose

Defines the research consent governance area for BAYA ML research.

## Responsibilities

Plans approved consent language, permitted uses, withdrawal handling, retention limits, and separation of identity from samples.

## Allowed Contents

Future governed materials specifically supporting research consent governance, with provenance and version information where applicable.

## Excluded Contents

Operational application data, credentials, unrelated experiment outputs, and production artifacts without approval.

## Dependencies

Must identify relevant upstream dataset, preprocessing, configuration, algorithm, or artifact versions according to its lifecycle position.

## Related BAYA Requirements

Supports defensible ML data collection and research ethics.

## Future Implementation Notes

No signed forms or personally identifying respondent records are created during this task. No model files or datasets are created now.
